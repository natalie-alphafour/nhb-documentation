# NHB Glossary

**Document type:** Official NHB terminology reference
**Applies to:** Any question about what a specific NHB term means — acronyms, feature names, program names, concepts, NHB-specific vocabulary
**Last updated:** April 2026

## Summary

NHB uses specific terminology for features, programs, plan tiers, and teaching concepts. This glossary is the quick reference for what each term means. When a member asks "what does X mean," MindAI should retrieve the definition here.

## Plan tiers and membership levels

**Free / NHB** — the entry-level tier, free to join. Limited access to most premium content. Display name updated from "Free" to "NHB" in March 2026.

**NHB Pro** — the core paid membership at $500 per month or $4,500 per year. Full access to NHB's content, community, weekly coaching calls, monthly Pro Hot Seats, and monthly Fast Feedback.

**Fast Forward (FF)** — NHB's flagship mastermind program. Direct hands-on access to Alen. $1,000 per month with a $10,000 down payment, or $20,000 per year paid in full. Renewals for existing FF members are $12,000 per year.

**Plus** — deprecated tier, no longer offered. Members should not expect Plus to be a current plan option. The current hierarchy is Free → Pro → Fast Forward.

**Coaching Program** — specialty add-ons on top of NHB Pro or Fast Forward. Each program is $1,000 per month. Each coaching program is led by one coach focused on one discipline.

**Meta Ads & Scale Lab (MASL)** — coaching program led by Loren Pinilis, focused on Facebook and Instagram advertising.

**Google Ads & Scaling Lab (GASL)** — coaching program led by Charles Kirkland, focused on Google Ads and paid traffic scaling.

**Live Launch (LL)** — coaching program led by Kevin Sheerin, focused on offer construction, product-market fit, and low-to-mid-ticket launches.

**CRO Accelerator (CROA)** — coaching program led by Brandon Vasquez, focused on conversion rate optimization.

**Email to CMO Pro** — coaching program led by Isaac Lara, focused on email marketing and the path from marketer to CMO.

**Copywriting Mastery** — coaching program led by Chris Haddad, focused on direct response copywriting, VSLs, and emotional storytelling.

**AI Superpower** — coaching program led by Robert Neckelius, focused on AI systems for marketing and business.

## Platform features

**Fast Feedback (FF)** — asynchronous personalized feedback from a coach. Members submit work via Google Doc, coach responds with a Loom video (typically). Note: "FF" can refer to either Fast Feedback or Fast Forward depending on context — always disambiguate from context.

**Hot Seat** — a live call format where a member's business, offer, or question is worked on in real time by a coach in front of the community. Members submit context via Google Doc and the coach discusses it on the scheduled call.

**Live Call / Live Calls** — scheduled Zoom sessions hosted by Alen, the coaching team, and guest speakers. All live calls are recorded.

**NHB Theory** — Alen's weekly deep-teaching call for NHB Pro members. Covers core NHB marketing and business thinking.

**FF Theory** — Alen's weekly deep-teaching call for Fast Forward members only. Goes deeper than NHB Theory into mastery-level material.

**FF Hot Seats** — Alen's weekly Hot Seat call for Fast Forward members only.

**NHB Coaching Call** — the umbrella term for each coach's weekly training call on their specialty (Meta Ads Scale Lab, Google Ads & Scaling Lab, and so on).

**Monthly NHB Pro Hot Seat** — each coach runs one per month. Replaces their regular weekly call that week. Open to any NHB Pro member who books a slot.

**Library** — searchable archive of every past call, Hot Seat, Fast Feedback response, course, and piece of long-form content produced inside NHB.

**Library of Truth Trainings** — each coach's curated set of trainings inside their coaching program's member vault. The name is the coach's own branding (Charles uses it, Brandon uses it, Loren uses it, others).

**Insights** — written articles and long-form content inside NHB.

**Resources** — practical assets like templates, swipe files, tools, and references. Separate from courses and live calls.

**Swipe files** — example ad creative, landing pages, sales letters, emails, and other copy assets members can reference and adapt. Alen's swipe stash is part of NHB's resources.

**Courses** — structured, step-by-step learning programs. Each coach has their own course curriculum inside their coaching program.

**Playlists** — curated ordered sequences of content pulled from the Library, organized around a specific learning path.

**Meetups** — in-person and virtual gatherings organized by NHB members and coaches.

**Forum** — NHB's native, long-form, searchable community discussion space on the platform.

**Discord** — NHB's live chat layer, running on the Discord platform. For real-time community conversation.

**Rolodex** — the searchable member directory inside the Connect section of NHB.

**MindAI** — the AI assistant built into NHB, trained on NHB content. Helps members find answers, navigate the library, and understand concepts.

**Bookmarks** — saved references to content inside NHB. Private to the member.

**Public Profile** — each member's profile page, visible to other members, showing bio, work, links, coaching programs, and activity.

**Home Dashboard** — the main landing page members see when they log into NHB.

**NHB Retreat** — NHB's flagship in-person experience, a separate offering from the membership and coaching programs.

## Teaching concepts and frameworks

**Socratic method** — NHB's core teaching approach. Teaches members how to think rather than handing out step-by-step systems, templates, or fill-in-the-blank formulas. Used by Alen and throughout NHB.

**P.I.G. Method (Punched In The Gut)** — Chris Haddad's signature copywriting framework. Focuses on deep empathy, psychological insight, and narrative technique to create copy that produces visceral emotional response.

**Compression Pricing** — Alen's term for pricing structures designed to filter for committed members. The Fast Forward down-payment structure is an example — the down payment filters for members who will stay long enough to get real value.

**Media Buyer's Media Buyer** — Charles Kirkland's industry nickname, reflecting his role as a media buyer who teaches other media buyers.

**Plan Subscription** — the data type used internally for member plan-level subscriptions (was formerly called "User Current Subscription" or "Current User Subscription" before being renamed).

**Coaching Subscription** — the data type used internally for coaching program subscriptions (was formerly called "CoachingSubscription").

**Subscription Source** — the field that tags subscription records as TC (ThriveCart), IS (InfusionSoft), or Manual depending on billing origin.

## Billing terminology

**TC** — ThriveCart. One of the two billing paths NHB uses. Legacy member subscriptions often run through ThriveCart.

**IS** — InfusionSoft. The primary billing path for most new member subscriptions, via NHB's custom IS API built by Umair.

**End of Cycle (EOC)** — when downgrades and cancellations take effect. Upgrades are immediate; downgrades are processed at the end of the current billing period.

## Organizational terminology

**NHB** — Nothing Held Back. The community and platform.

**AlphaFour** — Alen's company that operates NHB.

**NHB Mostar (NHBTEAM)** — the active internal team used for project management (Linear tickets).

## Content type terminology

**Post** — the universal library-facing record for any piece of content (live call, Hot Seat, Fast Feedback, forum thread, and more). Internal to NHB's data model.

**Access Level** — how a specific piece of content is gated by membership tier (Free, Pro, Fast Forward).

**Required Plan** — the minimum plan needed to access a specific piece of content.

## Summary for answering member questions

When a member asks "what does X mean" or about NHB terminology, the answer should come from this glossary. If the term is a feature or coach or program, refer to the specific dedicated document for full detail. If the term is a broader industry term (CRO, AOV, LTV), this glossary gives the short definition and points to where in NHB the concept is taught.

If a member asks about a term not in the glossary, MindAI should say so directly rather than guess. Members asking about unfamiliar terms are often looking for clarity, and a wrong definition is worse than admitting the term isn't covered.
