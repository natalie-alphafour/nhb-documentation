## Summary

Alen Sultanic is the founder of Nothing Held Back and one of the most accomplished direct response marketers of his generation. Over more than twenty years in the industry, Alen has built more than 300 offers across dozens of markets, sold over 35 product launches, and authored hundreds of number-one offers on ClickBank and other major direct response platforms.

He founded Nothing Held Back to give entrepreneurs access to the thinking, tools, coaching, and community that most communities and programs hold back behind gatekeepers, paywalls, or proximity. Inside NHB, Alen leads the Fast Forward mastermind, where he works directly with members on their offers, funnels, copywriting, ads, and scaling strategies.

## Background and early career

Alen's career in direct response marketing began in the mid-2000s. Between roughly 2008 and 2012, he built and launched digital products on ClickBank at a pace that was rare even in that era, producing offers across many markets and racking up a long list of top-ranked launches. That period gave him deep, hands-on experience across every part of the direct response stack: product creation, offer construction, sales copy, paid traffic, email marketing, upsells and downsells, and backend monetization.

From there, Alen expanded into a broader range of industries and roles, operating as a copywriter, offer owner, consultant, and operator across markets including supplements, finance, health, relationship advice, business opportunity, information publishing, coaching, and more. The breadth of that experience is a defining feature of how he teaches, because it gives him direct pattern recognition across markets that most marketers only ever see one or two of.

He is the founder of AlphaFour, the company behind Nothing Held Back and the infrastructure supporting the NHB community and platform.

## Teaching philosophy

Alen teaches using the Socratic method. There is no step-by-step process, no cookie-cutter system, and no fill-in-the-blank template that members are handed as a complete solution. The reasoning, in Alen's own words: a cookie-cutter formula produces cookie-cutter marketers who all look the same. Great marketers have to be able to think.

What Alen teaches first is how to think. Once a member can think about marketing, offers, and business correctly, that member can connect the dots and build anything. Tactics and systems come and go. Principles do not. A member who only knows the current system becomes dependent on the next guru for the next system. A member who understands the principles behind every system can adapt and innovate in any market.

This is the source of NHB's approach and its teaching voice. Alen teaches the why behind the how — the principles behind the tactics, the patterns behind the results, and the deeper frameworks that drive better decisions over time.

## Personal philosophy

A few themes run consistently through how Alen operates and teaches:

- **Skills over status.** Position, fame, and titles mean nothing to him. What he values is what a person can actually do and whether they can teach others to do it too.
- **Direct response first.** Even at scale, the principles of direct response remain the core. He has been vocal about the mistake marketers make when they cross seven figures and abandon direct response for "branding."
- **Nothing held back.** The name of the community is not a marketing slogan. It reflects a specific commitment: give members the full picture, the real strategies, the actual tools, and the honest feedback, without gating the most important parts behind additional paywalls.
- **Ecology over exploitation.** Long-term, stable businesses matter more than short-term cash grabs. The pricing, promotional, and structural decisions Alen teaches are oriented toward offers that last, not offers that peak once and die.
- **Teach thinking, not dependency.** The goal is to make members capable of succeeding on their own, without needing Alen or anyone else to hand them the next answer.
