## Summary

Loren Pinilis is a Meta ads specialist who combines the art of messaging with the science of data. He leads the Meta Ads & Scale Lab coaching program inside Nothing Held Back. His specialty is the nuance and complexity of what works in Meta advertising right now, tailored to a member's specific business and situation rather than one-size-fits-all playbooks copied from a single vertical.

Unlike marketers who lean on the same approach they used in their glory days ten years ago, Loren is still in the trenches daily across many niches, funnel types, and budget levels. That breadth of active work is the foundation of his teaching.

## Background and early career

Loren began his career in traditional marketing and public relations in 2006, handling the full spread of agency-side work: press releases, print design, radio and TV commercial production, social media, website design, and everything in between.

In 2016, he partnered with a friend to build an online fitness business. In that business he personally handled product creation, producing a podcast, funnel design and building, technology integration, copywriting, email marketing, and paid traffic. That hands-on role across the full stack of a direct response business is where his love for Meta ads began.

From there, Loren dove into Meta ads and landed agency work, where he had the opportunity to work with and learn from some of the most respected agency owners in direct response. That led to opportunities most Meta ad specialists only read about — including producing control-beating Facebook ad copy for Agora Financial.

Rather than chasing the gigs that paid the most, Loren has since deliberately chased the ones that were the most unusual, the most challenging, and the ones that produced the most new learning. The result is that he has worked — and had success — in nearly every niche, every funnel type, and every budget level.

## What Loren does best

Loren's own framing of his core capability is that Meta ads deserve more than a cookie-cutter approach. What he does best is look at all the variables — the offer, the budget level, the business, the niche, the ad account history — and turn that into a Meta ads system that actually produces results for that specific situation.

Four specific skill areas anchor his work:

- **Messaging and psychology.** Understanding what drives users to click and take action, and how to signal those triggers powerfully in the ad.
- **Algorithm harnessing.** Creating optimization and testing approaches that properly use the Meta algorithm, adapted to the specific offer and unit economics of each account.
- **Creative strategy.** Ideating and producing scroll-stopping images and videos that frame the message effectively.
- **Iteration and adaptation.** Knowing what data to analyze in order to continually optimize everything from creatives to copy to platform management.

## Teaching philosophy

Loren's core positioning is against two common failure modes in the paid traffic industry:

- **Glory days marketers.** Specialists who only talk about the accounts and tactics that worked for them ten years ago, without staying current in today's platform reality.
- **One-vertical cookie cutters.** Specialists who keep leaning on the same tired approach they learned in one niche or one company, without understanding why that advice might not work in a different context.

Loren's teaching is the opposite of both. He is still in the trenches daily, across many niches, funnel types, and budget levels. That lets him understand why a given piece of standard advice might work for some members and fail for others, and what to do instead.

His approach is built on matching the system to the situation. Every member's offer, budget, niche, and account history is different, and the Meta ads plan has to be built for that combination rather than copied from someone else's case study.
