## Summary

Kevin Sheerin is a business development strategist, offer builder, and operator who leads the Live Launch coaching program inside Nothing Held Back. Kevin has more than a decade of experience across online and offline business development and consulting, with a wide-ranging interest in the psychology, product development, marketing, and sales disciplines that determine whether an offer succeeds in the market.

His specialty is helping entrepreneurs build products and services that have high degrees of product-market fit before they spend a dollar on advertising. Rather than optimizing ads to compensate for a weak offer, Kevin teaches members to fix the offer itself first, which is where the compounding returns on any launch actually live.

## Background and early career

Kevin's career began in an unusual place. He started as a professional online poker player, earning over six figures during his final year of school. That early work gave him hands-on experience with risk, probability, decision-making under uncertainty, and the kind of pattern recognition that later shaped his work as a marketer and operator.

Since then, he has built more than a decade of experience in both online and offline business development and consulting, operating across a wide range of industries.

## Teaching philosophy

Kevin's approach is strategic and grounded in depth of knowledge across multiple disciplines. His distinguishing traits as a coach:

- **A strategic mind.** Kevin's value is in his ability to think through many factors and aspects of a business simultaneously — psychology, offer structure, copywriting, conversion mechanics, advertising, and pricing — and see how they interact before a launch happens.
- **Product first, marketing second.** His teaching repeatedly emphasizes that a weak product cannot be rescued by strong marketing. The work is to fix the offer first, and marketing will follow.
- **High attention to detail.** His training titles — Tangible vs Intangible Bumps, Self Evident vs Evaluative Offers, Primary & Secondary Payoffs — reflect the granular distinctions he teaches members to notice in their own offers.
- **Opportunity identification.** Kevin's consulting experience makes him particularly useful for members who need to quickly identify where the opportunity for new products or growth actually sits in their market.

His strategic mindset and breadth of knowledge make him a valuable coach for anyone looking to launch, develop, or scale a business — especially for entrepreneurs who want to get the offer right before they commit budget to paid acquisition.
