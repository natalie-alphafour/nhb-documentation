## Teaching philosophy

Robert's teaching is built on four pillars:

- **Execution over theory.** His stated format is 90% doing, 10% conceptual learning. The best way to learn AI for marketing, in his view, is to build, test, and iterate rather than consume endless content.
- **Systems thinking.** Rather than teaching AI tools in isolation, Robert teaches how to wire AI into existing marketing workflows, what specific tools to use for each task, and how to measure results.
- **Root cause thinking.** He writes and teaches about the core desires and motivations that drive human decision-making, and argues that misalignment between core motivations and stated goals creates self-sabotage in business owners.
- **Freedom as the north star.** His broader framing, visible across his public content, is helping people "start, grow, and eventually live off your online business so you can live a life of freedom." AI and automation are tools for reclaiming time and autonomy, not ends in themselves.

His signature promise for AI-powered marketing: "Get 10x more done with 1/10th the effort."
