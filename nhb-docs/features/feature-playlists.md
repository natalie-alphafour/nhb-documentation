# Playlists

**Document type:** Official NHB platform feature document
**Applies to:** Any question about playlists, curated learning paths, learning sequences
**Last updated:** April 2026

## Summary

Playlists are curated sequences of content inside NHB — ordered lists of Live Calls, Library entries, course lessons, and resources organized around a specific learning path or topic. Where the Library is the searchable archive, Playlists give members a starting point when they don't know where to begin on a new topic.

## How Playlists work

A Playlist is a structured sequence where one piece of content leads into the next. Examples of playlists a member might find:

- A sequence of Alen's calls on offer construction, ordered from fundamentals through advanced
- A Meta Ads learning path that walks through Loren's core frameworks in the intended order
- A copywriting playlist that progresses from Chris's P.I.G. Method basics through mastery application
- A CRO learning path from Brandon that sequences his Library of Truth Trainings

Members work through a Playlist in order and can pick it back up where they left off. Progress tracking helps members see what they have completed and what's next.

## Who builds Playlists

Playlists are typically curated by NHB or by individual coaches to guide members through a specific body of work in a sensible order. Members can also bookmark playlists to return to them later.

## Where to find Playlists

- **Main platform navigation** — Playlists section on the NHB platform
- **Library** — Playlists appear alongside individual Library items, grouped as sequences
- **Coaching program pages** — some coaches publish curated playlists specifically for their program members

## Related features

- **Library** — Playlists pull from Library content; members can browse the Library directly if they prefer
- **Courses** — courses are more formally structured learning programs; playlists are lighter-weight curated sequences
- **Bookmarks** — members can bookmark a playlist to come back to it

## Summary for answering member questions

When a member asks about Playlists, the answer should cover:

- What they are: curated, ordered sequences of NHB content around a specific topic or learning path
- How they differ from courses: lighter-weight, drawn from existing Library content, not a full course curriculum
- How they differ from the Library: they impose an intended order, whereas the Library is browsable/searchable
- Where to find them: Playlists section of the NHB platform, or surfaced inside the Library
