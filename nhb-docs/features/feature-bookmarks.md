# Bookmarks

**Document type:** Official NHB platform feature document
**Applies to:** Any question about bookmarks, saving content, coming back to saved items
**Last updated:** April 2026

## Summary

Bookmarks are how members save content inside NHB to come back to later. Any Library entry, forum thread, Fast Feedback submission, live call, course lesson, resource, or other piece of content can be bookmarked and pulled up later from the member's Bookmarks section.

## How Bookmarks work

A bookmark icon appears on most content inside NHB. Members click the bookmark icon to save the item. Clicking it again removes the bookmark.

Bookmarked items are collected in the member's personal Bookmarks view, where they can browse and filter by content type. Bookmarks are private to the member and do not appear to other users.

## What can be bookmarked

- Live Calls (upcoming and past)
- Library entries
- Course lessons
- Fast Feedback submissions and responses
- Hot Seats
- Forum threads
- Resources, templates, and swipe files
- Playlists
- Meetups

## Where to find Bookmarks

- **Main platform navigation** — Bookmarks section on the NHB platform
- **On each content page** — a bookmark toggle on individual content items
- **Fast Feedback page** — a dedicated Bookmarked tab

## Summary for answering member questions

When a member asks about Bookmarks, the answer should cover:

- What they are: saved references to content inside NHB, private to the member
- How to bookmark: click the bookmark icon on any content item
- How to access bookmarks: the Bookmarks section on the NHB platform, or the Bookmarked tab within specific features like Fast Feedback
- What can be bookmarked: most types of content inside NHB, including calls, Library entries, Fast Feedback, forum threads, courses, and resources
