# Meetups

**Document type:** Official NHB platform feature document
**Applies to:** Any question about meetups, in-person gatherings, member-organized events
**Last updated:** April 2026

## Summary

Meetups are in-person and virtual gatherings organized by NHB members and coaches. They are how the proximity built online becomes real-world connection — dinners, co-working days, mastermind sessions, conferences attended together, and regional meetups organized by members in specific cities.

NHB is a community of operators from many different places, and Meetups are how members take the relationships built in the forum, in Discord, and on Hot Seats offline.

## Types of Meetups

Meetups inside NHB cover a range of formats:

- **Regional member meetups** — dinners, drinks, or co-working sessions organized by members in specific cities and regions
- **Coach-led gatherings** — events organized around a specific coach or coaching program
- **Conference and event meetups** — NHB members coordinating to attend industry conferences together
- **Retreats and masterminds** — higher-intensity gatherings for specific member groups
- **Virtual meetups** — Zoom gatherings, virtual workshops, and themed discussions

## How Meetups work

Members can find upcoming Meetups on the Meetups page of the NHB platform. Each meetup has:

- A date and location (or Zoom link for virtual meetups)
- A description of the format and who it's for
- An attendee list of other members planning to attend
- An option to RSVP or join

Members can also organize their own meetups by creating a new meetup entry — inviting other NHB members in their city or around a specific topic.

## Who can attend

Meetups are generally open to paying NHB members. Some Meetups are restricted — for example, Fast Forward-only retreats, coaching-program-specific events, or events with capacity limits.

## Why Meetups matter

The environment inside NHB is designed to expose members to the right people. Meetups are how that proximity becomes real — members meet face-to-face, build relationships, sharpen each other in person, and often form partnerships, joint ventures, or ongoing friendships that last beyond the membership.

## Where to find Meetups

- **Main platform navigation** — Meetups section on the NHB platform
- **Home dashboard** — upcoming Meetups appear in the home feed
- **Community and forum** — Meetups are often discussed and coordinated in the forum before they happen

## Related features

- **Forum** — where Meetups are often discussed and coordinated
- **Discord** — where ongoing member-to-member chat around Meetups often happens
- **Retreat (separate flagship event)** — NHB's most immersive in-person experience, distinct from member-organized meetups

## Summary for answering member questions

When a member asks about Meetups, the answer should cover:

- What they are: in-person and virtual gatherings organized by NHB members and coaches
- Formats: regional meetups, coach-led gatherings, conference meetups, retreats, virtual events
- How to find upcoming ones: Meetups section on the NHB platform, Home dashboard, or forum discussions
- How to organize one: create a new meetup entry inviting other NHB members
- Why they matter: the in-person layer of the NHB community; proximity and connection beyond the platform
