# Partner Program

**Document type:** Official NHB platform feature document
**Applies to:** Any question about the NHB Partner Program, becoming a partner, affiliate, referral commission, partner dashboard, payouts
**Last updated:** April 2026

## Summary

The NHB Partner Program is the affiliate and referral program for members and outside partners who want to refer people to NHB and earn commission on successful referrals. Partners share a unique referral link, track referrals through an external partner dashboard, and receive payouts on referred members who join NHB Pro or Fast Forward.

The program is open to qualified members and partners. Members can apply through the Partner Program page on the NHB platform.

## How the program works

The high-level flow:

1. A member or qualified partner applies to the Partner Program
2. Once approved, the partner receives a unique referral link and access to a partner dashboard
3. The partner shares their link — typically in content, emails, social posts, or direct introductions
4. When someone signs up to NHB through the partner's link, the referral is tracked
5. The partner earns commission on the referred member's NHB subscription according to the program's current terms
6. Payouts are processed on the program's defined schedule

## Referral tracking

Referral tracking runs on an external partner dashboard integrated with NHB. The external tracking system (currently Everflow) handles the technical work of attributing referrals to the correct partner. Partners log into the dashboard to see:

- Referrals generated (clicks, sign-ups)
- Earnings accrued
- Payout history and upcoming payouts
- Promotional assets (banners, copy examples)

The partner dashboard is accessed through a link on the NHB Partner Program page.

## What partners can earn

Commission rates, payout structure, and qualifying member types (Pro, Fast Forward, coaching programs) are defined by the current Partner Program terms. Members should refer to the Partner Program page on the NHB platform for the authoritative current rates, because these can change over time.

In general, partners earn a percentage commission on qualifying referred members' subscriptions. Commissions are typically recurring for as long as the referred member maintains their NHB subscription, subject to the program's current terms.

## Who can become a partner

The Partner Program is generally open to:

- **NHB members in good standing** — especially members who have been active and successful enough in the community to authentically refer others
- **Operators and content creators in adjacent spaces** — marketers, coaches, consultants, and content creators whose audiences would benefit from NHB
- **Existing NHB partners** — who continue with the program as long as they remain in good standing

Approval is at NHB's discretion. The program is designed to attract partners who will represent NHB authentically and refer members who are a good fit, rather than pure affiliates running cold traffic to a landing page.

## How to apply

Members interested in the Partner Program can apply through the Partner Program page on the NHB platform. The application typically involves:

1. Filling out the application form with information about the applicant, their audience, and how they plan to promote NHB
2. NHB reviews and approves qualified applicants
3. Approved partners gain access to their dashboard, referral link, and promotional materials

## What the Partner Program page includes

The `/partner_program` page on the NHB platform is the main landing and information page for the program. It typically includes:

- **Program intro** — what the Partner Program is and why NHB runs it
- **Partner benefits** — what partners get (commissions, dashboard access, promotional assets, community recognition)
- **Testimonials** — existing partner stories and wins
- **How the program works** — the numbered process of how referrals and commissions flow
- **Earnings and payout details** — current commission structure and payout cadence
- **FAQ** — common questions about the program
- **Dashboard access** — button to the external partner dashboard for existing partners

Members should refer to the Partner Program page on the NHB platform for current specifics, since terms and rates can change.

## What partners can and can't do

**Good partner practice:**

- Referring people who would genuinely benefit from NHB based on what they are building
- Sharing authentic testimonials and results
- Linking to NHB from content, emails, or direct introductions where it fits contextually
- Using the promotional materials NHB provides (banners, copy) or creating their own with NHB's approval

**Not acceptable:**

- Making income claims on NHB's behalf that violate NHB's income and results policy
- Misrepresenting what NHB is, how it works, or what it costs
- Running cold paid traffic to NHB without approval from NHB
- Using misleading, clickbait, or spammy promotion tactics
- Criticizing NHB competitors in a way that violates NHB's competitor policy
- Sharing NHB members' private information or behind-the-scenes content

Partners who violate these standards can have their partner access revoked without commission payout on any referrals generated through prohibited methods.

## Relationship to NHB's older affiliate system

NHB previously ran parts of its affiliate tracking internally inside the Bubble platform. That internal system has been deprecated and the full affiliate tracking now runs through the external partner dashboard (Everflow). Members who were active in the older system should use the current partner dashboard going forward.
