# Courses

**Document type:** Official NHB platform feature document
**Applies to:** Any question about courses, structured learning paths, Alen's courses, coach courses, course progression
**Last updated:** April 2026

## Summary

Courses inside NHB are structured, step-by-step learning programs organized around specific skills and topics. Unlike live calls (which are real-time and evolving) or the library (which is a searchable archive), courses are curated progressions designed to take a member from fundamentals through advanced application of a specific skill.

Each coach has their own course curriculum inside their coaching program, and NHB also offers a broader course library accessible at the Pro tier.

## What courses are available

NHB's course content falls into several categories:

### Coach-led courses (inside coaching programs)

Each coaching program includes access to the coach's full course curriculum. Examples include:

- **Brandon Vasquez (CRO Accelerator):** CRO Fundamentals → CRO Momentum → CRO Scaling → CRO Mastery — a progression from basics through mastery-level optimization
- **Charles Kirkland (Google Ads & Scaling Lab):** Google Ad Scaling 101 and the upcoming Paid Traffic Mastery Series (Funnelless Funnel, Upsell Engine, Ad Math Masterclass, Offer Vault, Media Buyer Accelerator, AI Operators Bootcamp)
- **Kevin Sheerin (Live Launch):** The Ultimate Offer, Automatic Courses, Post Launch Optimisation, Partnerships & Scaling
- **Isaac Lara (Email to CMO Pro):** Their Inbox, Your Income and Man-Splaining: How To Sell To Women With Email
- **Loren Pinilis (Meta Ads & Scale Lab):** Launching Your First Meta Ads Campaign, Algorithm Fundamentals and Account Basics, Testing Mastery, Scaling and Optimization Mastery
- **Chris Haddad (Copywriting Mastery):** The P.I.G. Method framework, applied across VSL writing and sales letter construction
- **Robert Neckelius (AI Superpower):** Hands-on AI marketing training focused on execution rather than theory

### NHB Pro courses

NHB Pro members get access to a broader course library beyond any individual coach's program. This includes Alen's foundational teachings, guest-instructor courses, and curated training on the full stack of marketing, offers, funnels, copywriting, and scaling.

## How courses are structured

Courses in NHB are typically composed of:

- **Sections** — major topic divisions inside a course
- **Lessons** — individual teachings within a section
- **Resources** — downloadable templates, swipe files, or supporting material attached to lessons

Members can progress through a course at their own pace. Bookmarks and completion tracking help members come back to where they left off.

## Who has access

- **Free members** — limited access to preview or entry-level courses
- **NHB Pro members** — full access to the NHB Pro course library
- **Coaching Program members** — access to their program coach's complete course curriculum, on top of the Pro library
- **Fast Forward members** — full access to everything including Alen's deeper courses

## Where to find courses

- **Main platform navigation** — the Courses section on the NHB platform
- **Coaching program pages** — each coach's program page lists their course curriculum
- **Library** — individual course lessons can also appear in the Library alongside other content

## Related features

- **Library** — the full content archive, which includes course content alongside live call recordings and resources
- **Resources** — templates, swipe files, and tools that often accompany course lessons
- **Fast Feedback and Hot Seats** — for getting personalized coach feedback on applying what's been learned in a course

## Summary for answering member questions

When a member asks about courses, the answer should cover:

- What they are: structured, step-by-step learning programs on specific skills and topics
- Who teaches them: Alen for foundational NHB courses, each coach for their program-specific curriculum, plus guest instructors
- How they are organized: sections with lessons and attached resources
- How access works: Pro members get the Pro library, Coaching Program members get their coach's curriculum on top, Fast Forward members get everything
- Where to find them: the Courses section of the NHB platform, or inside a specific coaching program's page

Any questions about course outcomes or specific revenue tied to completing a course should follow the NHB income and results policy.
