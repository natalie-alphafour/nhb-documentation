# Account Management

**Document type:** Official NHB platform feature document
**Applies to:** Any question about managing an NHB account — logging in, password reset, updating payment info, changing plans, cancelling, adding team members, profile settings, billing history
**Last updated:** April 2026

## Summary

Account management covers everything a member does to administer their NHB membership outside of the actual content and community: signing in, updating payment info, changing plans, adding team members, cancelling, and managing profile settings. This doc is the quick reference for "how do I do X with my account."

## Logging in

Members log in at `app.nothingheldback.com` with the email and password they set during sign-up. Sessions persist across visits. If a member is logged out, they return to the login screen.

## Password reset

Members who have forgotten their password can reset it at `app.nothingheldback.com/auth?view=reset`. They enter the email associated with their NHB account and receive a reset link. The email comes from the NHB notification system (typically `support@nhbnotify.com`). Members who don't receive the reset email within a few minutes should check their spam folder.

If a member is unable to reset through the standard flow, NHB support can assist directly.

## Updating payment information

Members can update their payment method through:

- **Their NHB account settings** — typically under a Billing or Payment section
- **ThriveCart directly** — at `grow.thrivecart.com/updateinfo/` for members whose billing is on ThriveCart

Members should update their payment method **before** a failed charge to avoid interruption. If a charge fails, InfusionSoft retries up to 5 times; if all retries fail, the subscription cancels automatically and the member must re-subscribe to regain access.

## Changing plans

### Upgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately. A member upgrading from Free to NHB Pro, from NHB Pro to Fast Forward, or adding a coaching program gains access to the new tier or program at the moment of checkout.

Upgrade paths inside NHB:

- **Free → NHB Pro** — $500 per month or $4,500 per year
- **NHB Pro → Fast Forward** — $1,000 per month with a $10,000 down payment, or $20,000 per year
- **Adding a coaching program** — $1,000 per month as an add-on to an existing NHB Pro or Fast Forward membership

Members add or upgrade through the NHB platform's coaching section or through direct checkout links provided by NHB.

### Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the End of Cycle. A member who requests a downgrade mid-cycle keeps their higher-tier access until the current billing period ends, at which point the downgrade processes.

When a member downgrades their base membership to Free, all coaching program add-ons automatically cancel, because coaching programs require an active NHB Pro or Fast Forward membership.

## Cancelling a membership

Members can cancel at any time, month-to-month. There are no long-term contracts. Cancellation takes effect at the End of Cycle, with full access retained until that point.

Cancellation paths:

- **NHB platform account settings** — the primary path
- **ThriveCart** — for members on ThriveCart billing
- **NHB support** — for members who have difficulty cancelling through the platform

See the refund and cancellation policy document for the complete policy (no refunds, cancellation cascade for coaching programs, pause options).

## Adding team members

NHB supports adding team members for Pro and Fast Forward accounts, and for select coaching programs.

### NHB Pro team member

- **Cost:** $250 per month per additional team member
- **Access:** Team members get their own log-in under the primary member's account and full Pro-level access

### Fast Forward team member

- Separate "Additional FF Member" add-on available
- Team members get their own log-in and full Fast Forward access under the primary member's account

### Coaching program team members

Several coaching programs have team-member add-ons available through ThriveCart:

- Meta Ads & Scale Lab team member
- Google Ads & Scaling Lab team member
- Live Launch team member

Additional team members get program access under the primary member's account. Specific pricing per team member is handled at checkout.

## Profile settings

Members can configure their public profile through the account settings section:

- **Basic info** — name, profile photo, bio, location
- **Industry and niche** — market, sector, what they do
- **Work** — current projects, businesses, roles
- **Links** — social profiles, websites, external URLs
- **Visibility** — what appears on the public profile visible to other members
- **Color mode** — dark or light platform UI

See the Public Profile feature doc for full detail on what's displayed publicly.

## Notification settings

Members can configure which notifications they receive and through which channels (in-app, email) from the account settings. Common controls:

- Email notifications on or off for specific event types
- Follow/unfollow specific forum categories or members
- Mute specific threads or categories

See the Notifications feature doc for full detail.

## Invoices and billing history

Members can access their billing history and past invoices through their account settings. ThriveCart-billed members can also retrieve invoices directly from ThriveCart's member portal.

## Changing the email on an account

Members who need to change their primary email (for example, switching from a personal to a work email) can do so through account settings. Note that because the email is the login identifier, changing it also changes the member's log-in credentials. NHB support can assist if a member needs to change an email and can't do it through the standard flow.

## Account restoration and returns

A member who has cancelled and later wants to rejoin can do so by signing up again. Their previous profile and history may or may not be fully restored depending on the specific case — NHB support can help with account restoration where applicable.

## Summary for answering member questions

When a member asks about account management, the answer should cover:

- **Logging in:** `app.nothingheldback.com` with email and password
- **Password reset:** `app.nothingheldback.com/auth?view=reset`
- **Updating payment:** account settings or `grow.thrivecart.com/updateinfo/`
- **Upgrading:** through the NHB platform, takes effect immediately
- **Downgrading:** through the NHB platform or support, takes effect at End of Cycle
- **Cancelling:** through account settings, ThriveCart, or NHB support; End of Cycle timing
- **Team members:** NHB Pro $250/mo each, FF additional member available, select coaching programs support team seats
- **Profile settings:** configurable through account settings
- **No refunds:** all sales final, cancel before the next billing cycle

For anything account-related that the member cannot resolve through the platform, NHB support is the fallback path.
