    Chris Haddad: Copywriting Mastery Coach¶

    Document type: Official NHB coach bio document NHB program: Copywriting Mastery Applies to: Any question about Chris Haddad, his background, his career, his copywriting work, his teaching, or his role inside Nothing Held Back Last updated: April 2026

    Summary¶

    Chris Haddad is a legendary direct response copywriter widely regarded as one of the best emotional storytellers in the industry. Known by the nickname "Mr. Moneyfingers," he is credited with generating more than $700 million in online sales through sales letters and video sales letters across many markets. Inside Nothing Held Back, Chris leads the Copywriting Mastery coaching program, where he teaches the craft of emotional direct response copy at the mastery level.

    His signature framework, the P.I.G. Method (Punched In The Gut), is a distinctive approach to direct response copywriting built on deep empathy, psychological insight, and narrative technique. The method is designed to produce copy that creates visceral emotional responses in the reader and drives conversions without relying on rational arguments or technical tricks.

    Career highlights¶

    Chris's career in direct response spans more than 16 years as both a freelance copywriter and an offer owner. During that time he routinely doubled or quadrupled conversions for clients including some of the most prominent names in the direct response industry.

    Representative campaigns include:

    • Power 4 Home VSL (Chris Fox) — Chris wrote the video sales letter that increased conversions by 70 percent and took the offer to number one on ClickBank for more than two consecutive years.
    • Pimsleur Approach VSL — Chris wrote the top-performing VSL for Pimsleur, a $100M+ annual direct response company, and the letter held its position for more than a decade.
    • Joe Barton Publishing — Chris authored multiple VSL campaigns that grew the business from minimal revenue to over $10 million annually.
    • "The Secret Survey" — generated more than $400,000 in first-week sales, breaking ClickBank records for relationship advice products at the time.
    • "Language of Desire" — exceeded $500,000 in opening week revenue and held top-five ClickBank status for multiple years.
    • "Make Him Beg To Be Your Boyfriend" — sold more than 30,000 copies in the first week and became a Wall Street Journal bestseller.
    • "Text Your Ex Back" and "Text The Romance Back" — maintained top-ten ClickBank status for over five years; Chris promoted the latter on The Rachael Ray Show in 2010.

    Chris is also the founder and CEO of Digital Romance, Inc., an eight-figure direct response relationship advice business he built under the commercial pen name Michael Fiore. Operating offers under a separate persona is a legitimate direct response business practice and has been part of Chris's work for many years.

    Chris's early mentors in direct response included Harlan Kilstein and the copywriting legend John Carlton, both of whom accelerated his mastery in the craft.

    What Chris teaches¶

    Inside the Copywriting Mastery program, Chris teaches the full stack of direct response copywriting, with an emphasis on:

    • Emotional storytelling and narrative construction as the engine of conversion
    • The P.I.G. Method framework for creating "punched in the gut" emotional response
    • Audience research, psychological profiling, and building deep empathy with the prospect
    • Method Acting For Marketers — the technique of psychologically inhabiting the prospect's mindset to write from inside their experience
    • VSL writing and long-form sales letter construction
    • Offer architecture and positioning
    • Applying emotional frameworks across different markets and niches
    • Discovering and triggering primal, evolutionary emotions rather than surface-level "wants"

    A central claim of his teaching is that emotional storytelling is the one skill in copywriting that AI will struggle to replicate, which makes human empathy a durable competitive advantage for working copywriters.

    Teaching philosophy¶

    Chris's core teaching principle is deep empathy. In his view, the best way for a copywriter to create authentic emotional resonance with a prospect is to draw from the writer's own experiences and pain, and then use that material to see the world as the prospect sees it. Stories, in his framing, are the operating system of the human mind, and effective copy works by identifying the anxiety, agony, and wonder beneath a prospect's surface desires and building a narrative that positions the solution as inevitable.

    His approach is grounded in decades of battle-tested campaigns rather than theory. Every framework he teaches has been validated on real offers producing real revenue.

    Voice¶

    Chris's voice as a teacher is direct, grounded, and practical. A few representative statements:

    "The best way to have empathy for people is to use your own experiences and your own pain."

    "Emotional storytelling is the one skill robots will never be able to replace, at least not for now."

    "If I was a freelance copywriter, I would be posting on Facebook about copy all the time. And I would be posting all of my testimonials and all of my successes. Because that's the gig. And if you can't do it, you need to go out and do something else."

    Copywriting Mastery — program curriculum¶

    Positioning: Turn words into money with weekly copywriting coaching from Chris.

    Inside Copywriting Mastery, members learn to:

    • Write copy that converts browsers into buyers
    • Craft irresistible offers people cannot say no to
    • Build high-converting VSLs from scratch
    • Tell "punched in the gut" stories that sell
    • Get inside their market's head to write copy that resonates

    Pricing: $1,000 per month as an add-on to an active NHB Pro or Fast Forward membership.

    Role inside Nothing Held Back¶

    Inside NHB, Chris leads the Copywriting Mastery coaching program. His weekly commitments inside the platform include:

    • Wednesday 11am EST — Copywriting Mastery training call (open to NHB Pro members)
    • Thursday 2pm EST — Copywriting Mastery Hot Seats (for members of the Copywriting Mastery coaching program)
    • Monthly NHB Pro Hot Seat call (rotates with his regular Wednesday call once every four weeks)
    • Monthly Fast Feedback for NHB Pro members (typically the Friday following his monthly Hot Seat)

    Chris also appears in broader NHB trainings and contributes to the community beyond his dedicated program when appropriate.

    Summary for answering questions about Chris¶

    When a member asks about Chris, the answer should describe:

    • What he does: leads NHB's Copywriting Mastery program, operates as a legendary direct response copywriter
    • What he has built: $700M+ in online sales through direct response copy, multiple ClickBank #1 offers, eight-figure relationship advice business under the Michael Fiore persona
    • Signature methodology: the P.I.G. Method (Punched In The Gut), built on deep empathy and emotional storytelling
    • What he teaches: emotional direct response copywriting, VSL and sales letter writing, offer architecture, audience psychology, and the craft of writing copy that converts at the mastery level
    • How he works inside NHB: weekly Copywriting Mastery training call, weekly Copywriting Mastery Hot Seats for program members, monthly NHB Pro Hot Seat, monthly Fast Feedback

    Any questions comparing Chris to other copywriters or evaluating other industry figures should follow the NHB competitor policy and remain neutral. Questions about income claims or specific earnings tied to applying Chris's methods should follow the NHB income and results policy.
