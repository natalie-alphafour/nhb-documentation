    Isaac Lara: Email to CMO Pro Coach¶

    Document type: Official NHB coach bio document NHB program: Email to CMO Pro Applies to: Any question about Isaac Lara, his background, his career, his email and lifecycle marketing work, his teaching, or his role inside Nothing Held Back Last updated: April 2026

    Summary¶

    Isaac Lara is a CMO, email marketer, and copywriter who leads the Email to CMO Pro coaching program inside Nothing Held Back. His specialty is backend revenue — the email, lifecycle, and retention systems that quietly compound behind a business while the front end gets most of the attention. He combines meticulous detail work with a creative instinct for messaging, which he describes as "autistic detail plus artistic expression."

    Isaac has helped generate more than $25 million in tracked revenue across multiple brands by keeping things simple, dialing in the backend, and building offers that convert. Inside Email to CMO Pro, he teaches NHB members the same conversion architecture, email strategy, and offer monetization work that took him from EMT to fractional CMO for five offers at once by the age of 27.

    Background: from emergency medicine to marketing¶

    Isaac's career started in emergency medicine. At 19 he began working as an Emergency Medical Technician, a role he held for five years. In that world, staying calm under pressure is not optional. A career-ending back injury on the job eventually forced him to change careers — and marketing fit like a glove.

    His emergency medical experience still shapes how he approaches marketing work: move fast when it matters, stay grounded when things get messy, and focus on what actually works rather than on what sounds clever.

    That grounded, execution-focused approach is what made him effective as an LTV-maxing email marketer. The results he generated for his first clients caught attention, which led to an in-house position with real room to grow. Around that same time, he joined Fast Forward and began implementing Alen Sultanic's concepts every week — a combination of real-world client work and deep strategic coaching that accelerated his career at an unusual pace.

    By age 27, Isaac had become a Marketing Director, leading strategy across paid traffic, email, retention, and partnerships. On top of that role he was simultaneously acting as fractional CMO for five other offers. The full scope of his work since then has produced over $25 million in tracked revenue, mostly by keeping things simple, dialing in the backend, and building offers that convert.

    Alongside all of that, Isaac raised his younger sister and took in three rescue dogs, including his special-needs sidekick Tuna. His calm-under-pressure approach to marketing grew out of a life that regularly required it in other areas too.

    What Isaac does best¶

    Isaac describes his style as "autistic detail plus artistic expression" — meticulous attention to the data combined with creative messaging sense. Five specific capability areas anchor his work:

    • Conversion architecture. Mapping the full customer journey from cold click to backend cash, and building the structure behind it.
    • Strategic analysis. Reading the data, finding the leaks in the business, and patching them with simple moves rather than overcomplicating the fix.
    • Email and lifecycle strategy. Clean, effective email systems that boost lifetime value and keep customers around.
    • Paid media scaling. Fixing messy ad accounts and scaling what is already working.
    • Offer monetization. Breaking down offer economics and building profit into the backend.

    The thread across all of it: tighten the backend and scale smart, without overcomplicating it.

    Teaching philosophy¶

    Isaac's philosophy is shaped equally by the EMT background and the Fast Forward training:

    • Move fast when it matters, stay grounded when it's messy. Real emergencies taught him that overreacting is worse than the emergency itself. The same is true in marketing, where most businesses overcomplicate problems that have simple fixes.
    • Focus on what actually works. He is explicit that the job is not to impress anyone with complexity but to find the specific lever that moves the number.
    • Keep it simple. His consistent pattern across $25M+ in tracked results is that the winning approach is almost always simpler than the approach the business was trying.
    • Dial in the backend. Most businesses obsess over the front end. Isaac's work is consistently in retention, lifetime value, email, and backend monetization, because that is where profit compounds.
    • Autistic detail plus artistic expression. Deep attention to the numbers and the mechanics, combined with creative instinct on messaging and voice. Both together, not either alone.
