    Brandon Vasquez: CRO Accelerator Coach¶

    Document type: Official NHB coach bio document NHB program: CRO Accelerator Applies to: Any question about Brandon Vasquez, his background, his career, his conversion rate optimization work, his teaching, or his role inside Nothing Held Back Last updated: April 2026

    Summary¶

    Brandon Vasquez is a full-funnel CRO strategist and revenue optimizer who leads the CRO Accelerator coaching program inside Nothing Held Back. He describes himself as relentlessly data-driven since day one, with a focus on turning raw buyer behavior into repeatable, predictable revenue. Today Brandon manages more than $50 million in annual CRO-driven revenue and builds optimization systems for brands spending up to $50,000 per day on paid traffic.

    His specialty is diagnosing and optimizing every stage of a marketing funnel, from the initial click through backend and upsells, so that businesses extract more revenue from the traffic they are already driving rather than relying on more ad spend.

    Background and early career¶

    Brandon's path into marketing started at the University of Texas at Austin, where he earned a degree in advertising and developed an obsession with influence, human behavior, and what actually drives action. He went deep on persuasion, media, and PR coursework during his time there.

    Alongside his academic training, his real education in "people handling" came from managing a restaurant, where he engaged daily with people from every background and learned to quickly read what drives decisions. That instinct became the foundation of his later work as a marketer.

    After graduating, Brandon jumped into copywriting and built his portfolio through freelance work on Upwork and Fiverr. That early experience led to a full-time role at a digital ad agency, where he wrote ad copy for major accounts, authority figures, and thought leaders with large audiences across coaching, consulting, and education. Several of his campaigns became the control — the benchmark that other versions had to beat — for launches that reached millions of people.

    Copywriting was his entry point into the industry, but he quickly wanted to influence more than just the copy on a page. He wanted to shape the entire customer journey, from the first click through upsells and high-ticket backend offers. That ambition pushed him into conversion rate optimization, where he could refine every stage of the funnel, maximize the impact of every advertising dollar, and turn more leads into long-term customers.

    Major roles and results¶

    Brandon's career since then has included:

    • Copy Chief and Head of CRO at one of the largest brands in online art education, where he drove seven-figure lifts by optimizing funnels, storefronts, and email strategies
    • Consulting for industry leaders in e-commerce, direct-to-consumer, SaaS, and lead generation in the finance space — taking on crowded and brutally competitive markets
    • Currently managing $50M+ in annual CRO-driven revenue across multiple accounts
    • Building optimization systems for brands spending up to $50,000 per day on ads

    He positions his own work as being a "data detective" — cracking the code behind buyer behavior and turning every insight into repeatable profit.

    What Brandon teaches¶

    Inside the CRO Accelerator program, Brandon's teaching covers the full stack of conversion optimization. His approach is to maximize the value of every click, visit, and customer, so members get more revenue from the traffic they are already driving rather than having to buy more.

    Core focus areas:

    • Maximizing the value of every click, visit, and customer
    • Structuring split tests around what actually moves the needle — offer, layout, messaging, and user flow — not surface-level tweaks
    • Building reliable systems that improve conversions, increase average order value (AOV), and make funnels perform rather than just look good

    Library of Truth Trainings (available inside the CRO Accelerator member vault):

    • Anatomy of High-Converting Landing Pages — what actually makes a landing page perform and what is just noise
    • Market Voice to Market Fit — matching messaging to what an audience actually wants to hear
    • The CRO Audit & Roadmap Playbook — a step-by-step process for auditing funnels and building a prioritized testing roadmap
    • Heatmap Mastery — uncovering hidden user behaviors and bottlenecks through heatmap analysis
    • Competition Researcher Decoder — how to read competitor funnels, positioning, and traffic to find gaps and opportunities

    Upcoming trainings in development:

    • Page Friction Fixers — eliminating the hidden points of resistance that cost conversions
    • Retention Rocket Fuel — strategies that keep customers buying and coming back
    • Scrollmap Secrets — reading scrollmaps for deeper user insight and smarter page optimization
    • Above-the-Fold Gold — what belongs above the fold to hook, hold, and convert
    • AOV Boosters — tactics to increase average order value without killing conversion rate

    Course progression inside CRO Accelerator:

    • CRO Fundamentals — master the basics and set a funnel up for higher conversions
    • CRO Momentum — tactics and strategies that accelerate growth and stack quick, compounding wins
    • CRO Scaling — advanced strategies that break plateaus and drive sustainable, systemized growth at scale
    • CRO Mastery — the art and science of relentless optimization, building growth engines that outperform and outlast the competition

    Teaching philosophy¶

    Brandon's philosophy is that when the numbers are understood, luck becomes irrelevant. In his own words: "When you know the numbers, you don't need luck. You just need a plan."

    His core principles:

    • Full-funnel perspective. Every step from the first click to the upsell sequence matters. No single stage is too small to test and optimize.
    • Data as the foundation. Decisions come from disciplined split testing and buyer behavior analysis, not from guessing or from what other people say is working.
    • Structure tests around what moves the needle. Offer, layout, messaging, and user flow are where the real lifts live — not the color of a button.
    • Scalable and repeatable. The goal is systems where scalability and ROI are both predictable and repeatable, not one-off wins.
    • Audience-first thinking. Matching market voice to market fit — building detailed customer understanding before writing copy or designing flows — ensures alignment between message and buyer.

    Voice¶

    Brandon's voice as a teacher is direct, grounded in numbers, and pragmatic:

    "For me, it's about being a data detective and cracking the code behind buyer behavior so I can turn every insight into profit on repeat."

    "When you know the numbers, you don't need luck. You just need a plan."

    "If you want to improve results without spending more on traffic, I'll show you how to turn underperforming funnels into scalable, high-revenue systems."

    • Uncover hidden revenue in their existing funnel
    • Run split tests that actually move the needle
    • Lower their CPA while scaling revenue
    • Build data-backed testing strategies
    • Troubleshoot revenue bottlenecks fast

    • Friday 2pm EST — CRO Adventures training call (open to NHB Pro members)
    • Friday 10am EST — CROA Hot Seats (for members of the CRO Accelerator coaching program)
    • Monthly NHB Pro Hot Seat call (rotates with his Friday training call once every four weeks)
    • Monthly Fast Feedback for NHB Pro members (typically the Tuesday following his monthly Hot Seat)
