    Nothing Held Back: Overview¶

    Document type: Official NHB overview Applies to: Any question about what NHB is, what it does, who it is for, what it offers, or how it works Last updated: April 2026

    What Nothing Held Back is¶

    Nothing Held Back (NHB) is a transformational environment built for entrepreneurs who want to develop the thinking, skills, and standards required to build at the highest level. It is founded by Alen Sultanic.

    NHB is not a content library, a networking group, or a motivational community. It is an environment designed to change how members think, how they operate, and what they build. The platform, the trainings, the coaches, the community, and the tools are the mechanism. The real product is the transformation of the entrepreneur using them.

    The name reflects the operating principle. Most communities and programs hold back the strategies, frameworks, tools, and connections that matter most, keeping them gated, diluted, or behind proximity most members cannot access. NHB holds nothing back. Because when nothing is held back from a member, nothing can hold that member back.

    The core thesis¶

    Most entrepreneurs are not held back by effort. They are held back by the limits of what they believe is possible and by the environments they operate in. Those limits get formed long before anyone starts a business, shaped by the people around them, the ideas they were exposed to, and the version of "successful" they inherited.

    A member can work harder inside a limited frame, but they cannot build beyond it until the frame itself changes.

    NHB is built around three forces that change the frame:

    1. A new way of thinking — exposure to ideas, principles, and mental models that expand what a member believes is possible and how they approach problems.
    2. A powerful environment — a space that makes a higher standard feel like the baseline, creates forward momentum, and exposes blind spots.
    3. The right people — proximity to coaches and members who operate at a higher level, whose standards raise the member's standards simply by being in the same room.

    When these three forces compound, growth starts to build on itself. Results that once felt exceptional start to become the baseline.

    How NHB teaches¶

    NHB's teaching approach is Socratic. Members are not handed step-by-step processes, fill-in-the-blank templates, or cookie-cutter formulas. The goal is to teach members how to think about marketing, offers, and business, so they can connect the dots and build anything.

    The reasoning is simple. Systems and templates go out of date. Principles do not. A member who only knows a system becomes dependent on the next system when the first one stops working. A member who understands the principles behind every system can adapt, rebuild, and innovate in any market under any conditions.

    This is why NHB teaches the why behind the how. The principles behind the tactics. The patterns behind the results. The frameworks that drive better decisions over time.

    What gets covered¶

    NHB covers the full stack of modern digital marketing and business building, including:

    • Offers and offer architecture
    • Funnels and funnel economics
    • Copywriting and sales letters
    • Paid advertising across Meta, Google, YouTube, TikTok, X, and other channels
    • Conversion rate optimization and creative strategy
    • Email marketing and backend sequences
    • Webinars, challenges, live launches, and book-a-call funnels
    • Scaling and media buying
    • Positioning, pricing, and market selection
    • Brand, storytelling, and voice
    • Team building and hiring
    • The psychology of selling, buying, and influence

    Each topic is taught not as a standalone tactic but as part of an integrated system. The thinking behind one discipline connects to the thinking behind the others, which is what allows a member to operate as a full-stack marketer rather than a specialist trapped in a single channel.

    What is inside the platform¶

    NHB is organized into four functional areas that map to how members actually work:

    Learn¶

    • Live Calls — Weekly training calls with Alen and the coaching team, plus guest speakers. Live calls are where new ideas are introduced and discussed in real time.
    • Library — A searchable archive of every call, training, and insight, organized by coach, topic, and level.
    • Courses — Structured learning paths for specific skills and topics.
    • Insights — Written articles and frameworks for deeper reading.

    Build¶

    • Templates — Offer frameworks, funnel structures, and copy templates used by coaches and top members.
    • Swipe Files — Ad creative, landing pages, emails, and sales letters across many markets and formats.
    • Tools — Practical tools designed to shorten the execution curve.

    Get Help¶

    • Fast Feedback — Members submit their work (copy, ads, offers, funnels, videos) and get direct feedback from coaches, typically delivered via Loom video.
    • Hot Seats — Live sessions where members bring a real problem to a coach and work through it in front of the community.
    • MindAI — An AI assistant trained on NHB's content, available to help members find answers, frameworks, and resources inside the platform.

    Connect¶

    • Forum — The community's main discussion space on the NHB platform, where members post questions, share wins, and discuss what they are building.
    • Discord — A live chat layer for real-time conversation between members and coaches.
    • Meetups — In-person and virtual gatherings where members meet, build, and sharpen each other.

    NHB is gradually transitioning member-to-member discussion onto the NHB platform's own forum, with Discord remaining for live chat. The Facebook group still exists but is being phased out as the platform matures.

    Who teaches¶

    NHB's coaches are operators actively working on offers and businesses in real markets. They are not career educators teaching theory from the sidelines. Each coach leads a specific coaching program focused on their area of expertise, and every coach offers weekly training calls, monthly Hot Seats, and monthly Fast Feedback opportunities for members inside the NHB platform.

    The coaching lineup:

    • Alen Sultanic — Fast Forward. Founder of NHB. Legendary direct response marketer with 20+ years in the game, 300+ offers built, and hundreds of top-ranked ClickBank launches. Leads Fast Forward, NHB's highest-intensity mastermind, where he works hands-on with members on offers, funnels, copywriting, ads, and scaling. Also teaches across the broader NHB membership.
    • Chris Haddad — Copywriting Mastery. One of the most respected copywriters in the direct response world. Leads the Copywriting Mastery program, teaching members the craft of persuasive writing at the mastery level.
    • Charles Kirkland — Google Ads & Scaling Lab. Known in the industry as "the Media Buyer's Media Buyer." Leads the Google Ads & Scaling Lab, teaching members Google Ads strategy and how to scale paid traffic effectively.
    • Loren Pinilis — Meta Ads & Scale Lab. Specializes in Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising. Leads the Meta Ads & Scale Lab program, teaching members how to build, run, and scale Meta campaigns.
    • Kevin Sheerin — Live Launch. Specializes in low-ticket offer launches and the structure required to launch them successfully. Leads the Live Launch program.
    • Brandon Vasquez — CRO Accelerator. Specializes in conversion rate optimization. Leads the CRO Accelerator program, teaching members how to systematically improve conversion on funnels, pages, and offers.
    • Isaac Lara — Email to CMO Pro. Specializes in email marketing and the broader path from copywriter or marketer into CMO-level roles. Leads the Email to CMO Pro program.
    • Robert Neckelius — AI Superpower. Specializes in AI-driven marketing, creative, and workflow. Leads the AI Superpower program, teaching members how to leverage AI tools for marketing output, research, and execution.

    Each coach's individual background, program scope, and teaching specifics are covered in the dedicated coach documents inside the MindAI knowledge base. NHB also brings in guest speakers and working experts to share what they are seeing and learning in real time.

    The community¶

    The NHB community spans every stage of the entrepreneurial journey. Members include new builders, copywriters, freelancers, agency owners, offer owners, and operators running businesses at every revenue level, from first six figures through nine figures. Members come from many industries, including supplements, finance, coaching, e-commerce, software, health, education, real estate, entertainment, and more.

    What the community shares is not a revenue number or a business model. It is a standard for how members think, how they operate, and what they are willing to accept from themselves. That shared standard is what makes the community function as an environment rather than just a group.

    Seven-, eight-, and nine-figure operators are in the community alongside new members. Experienced members share what they know, new members contribute their questions and perspectives, and everyone benefits from the proximity. The community is active every day, in every channel.

    Who NHB is for¶

    NHB is built for entrepreneurs who know there is more in them than what they have currently built. It is for members who want the thinking, the skills, the environment, and the people required to move beyond where they are.

    NHB is specifically built for:

    • Copywriters who want to become offer owners, CROs, or CMOs
    • Offer owners who want to scale, refine their economics, or expand into new markets
    • Agency owners who want to build better offers for their clients and grow their own businesses
    • Coaches and course creators who want to build stronger offers and scale their reach
    • Founders running e-commerce, software, or information businesses who want better marketing skills in-house
    • Freelancers who want to develop deeper skills and take on higher-value work
    • Marketers at any stage who want to understand the full stack rather than specialize narrowly
    • Entrepreneurs tired of piecing together strategy from scattered podcasts, courses, and coaches

    It is for members willing to learn, willing to be challenged, willing to apply what they learn, and willing to do the work consistently over time.

    Who NHB is not for¶

    NHB is not for members looking for:

    • Fill-in-the-blank templates as a complete solution
    • Guaranteed results or easy answers
    • A content library they can graze and forget
    • Hype, motivation, or inspiration without substance
    • Networking without the expectation of building

    The Socratic teaching approach means members have to think, apply, and iterate. NHB does not provide step-by-step instructions that remove the thinking from the process, because that is precisely the approach that produces dependent, interchangeable marketers rather than operators who can build anything.

    How NHB is structured as a membership¶

    NHB offers multiple membership levels, designed to match different stages of a member's journey and different levels of access to coaches.

    • A free tier gives new members limited access to explore the platform and see how it works.
    • A paid membership level opens full access to training, the library, the community, and monthly feedback opportunities with coaches.
    • A higher-intensity mastermind program provides direct, hands-on access to Alen and weekly deep-work sessions on member offers and businesses.

    Specific pricing, features, and current plan names should always be verified against the live platform, since the membership structure evolves.

    What transformation looks like inside NHB¶

    Members who engage fully with NHB tend to experience change across several dimensions over time:

    • Thinking — They see their market, their offer, and their own business more clearly. They stop guessing. They start connecting dots that were invisible before.
    • Execution — They move faster, with more intention, and waste less effort on the wrong things.
    • Offers and positioning — Their offers get sharper. Their messaging gets more specific. Their positioning attracts better customers.
    • Business outcomes — Growth compounds. New opportunities appear. Existing constraints loosen.
    • Standards — What the member is willing to accept from themselves rises. What felt like a ceiling starts to look like a floor.
    • Identity — The member becomes someone different. Not because they were told to, but because the environment made it the natural path.

    NHB cannot promise any of these outcomes for any individual member, because outcomes depend entirely on what the member brings to the experience and what they do with it. What NHB can provide is the thinking, the environment, the people, the coaching, and the tools designed to make all of those outcomes possible.

    Summary¶

    Nothing Held Back is a transformational environment for entrepreneurs. It combines training, coaching, Fast Feedback, Hot Seats, community, and tools to help members develop the thinking, skills, and standards required to build at the highest level. The teaching approach is Socratic, focused on principles and mental models rather than templates and systems. The community spans every stage and many industries. The coaches are operators actively working on real businesses. The environment is designed to make a higher standard feel normal, so members rise to it naturally over time.

    The name reflects the core commitment. Because when nothing is held back from a member, nothing can hold that member back.
